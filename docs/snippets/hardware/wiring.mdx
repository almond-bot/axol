### Wiring

Follow this section if you have the **Camera Kit** and a **Compute Kit**.

Place the computer and the power supply near Axol, then connect the cables:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Position the computer">
    Set the computer close enough that the camera, USB and power cables reach the back of Axol.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the head camera">
    Run the head camera's **GMSL** cable from the camera to a GMSL2 input on the computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the wrist cameras">
    Connect each wrist camera to the GMSL port on its wrist with a short right-angle **GMSL** cable. Then run a long GMSL cable from each wrist-camera port on the back of Axol to a GMSL2 input on the computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect USB and power">
    Run the **USB** cable from the back of Axol to the computer. Plug the computer into the DC adapter that ships with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
