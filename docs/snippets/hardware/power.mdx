### Power

Axol runs on **24V DC, 60A peak** through the XT90 connector on its back. Almond offers two power supplies. Both are 1500W (24V, 62.5A) with an XT90 output:

- **[Standard Power Supply](https://www.almond.bot/standard-power-supply)**: the desktop supply that ships with Axol.
- **[Industrial Power Supply](https://www.almond.bot/industrial-power-supply)**: for permanent installations. It adds an **E-Stop**, a **power switch**, and a **brake resistor** that safely absorbs the current the arms send back.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Robot input** | 24V DC, 60A peak, XT90 |
| **Supply output** | 1500W (24V, 62.5A) |
| **DC cable** | 1.5m XT90 male–female |
| **AC** | Power-supply AC cord |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place the power supply">
    Set the supply close enough that the XT90 cable reaches the back of Axol.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect DC">
    Plug the XT90 cable from Axol into the power supply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the E-Stop (Industrial Power Supply only)">
    Plug the E-Stop into the supply and put it where the operator can reach it. The supply does not turn on without the E-Stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect AC and power on">
    Plug the supply's AC cord into a wall outlet, then switch the supply on. On the Industrial Power Supply, press the power switch to power Axol.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On the Industrial Power Supply, the E-Stop cuts power to the arms at once. To restore power, release the E-Stop and press the power switch again.
</Note>

<Note>
  The supply draws up to 1500W. Plug it into an outlet with nothing else on it.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The Compute Kit has its own power. Use the DC adapter that ships with it. The ZED Box Orin takes **12–19V**, up to 60W.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Cutting power to a running Jetson can corrupt its operating system. Shut it down from the operating system first, then unplug its adapter.
</Note>
