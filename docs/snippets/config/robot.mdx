<Accordion title="Controller and command rates">
  **Position controller.** `controller` `position` sets every joint to its firmware position loop (`a4` on the MyActuator joints, `pv` on the Damiao wrists) and uses the `firmware.*` gains. It is stiff: there is no compliance and no host feed-forward. Teleop takes `--axol.controller position`, and recorded-motion replays take `--controller` ([`tune.motion`](/cli/tune-motion)).

  At 400 Hz the bus can't carry every motor every tick:

  - The MyActuator joints are commanded every tick.
  - The two wrists are commanded on alternate ticks, 200 Hz each.
  - One precise position read per tick rotates through the MyActuator joints and the gripper, about 67 Hz each.

  **Impedance rates.** Impedance joints run at 240 Hz. An arm that mixes impedance joints with firmware-loop joints runs its core at 480 Hz, with each impedance joint on alternate ticks. Any other core rate with an impedance joint on the arm is refused. Under `impedance`, a `wire_mode` you set on a joint is kept.

  With `impedance_hz` at `480.0`, or any joint's own `impedance_hz` at `480.0`, the arm's core runs at 480 Hz. The 480 Hz joints are commanded every tick, and the other impedance joints, including the Damiao wrists, on alternate ticks. `tune.motion --impedance-hz 480` and `tune.motion --fast-impedance right.shoulder_1` set these for one run.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Firmware position loops (a4 and pv)">
  - A joint on `a4` or `pv` has no compliance and no host feed-forward.
  - An `a4` joint's torque reads NaN, so the contact stop can't see that joint. A `pv` wrist keeps its torque reading.
  - The gripper, gravity compensation and the limp fallback always use `mit`. On X6-P20 motors with the 2025-07 firmware, an `a4` joint that was hand-guided needs a re-enable before it tracks again.
  - On `pv`, the core sets the Damiao control mode at bring-up and switches it back to MIT for limp and gravity compensation.

  **Firmware gains.** The `firmware.*` fields are the motor's own position and speed loops. They act only while a joint runs a firmware loop, so every joint leaves them `null` by default and bring-up keeps the motors' stored values. When set, a gain is written to the motor's ROM at enable. Find values with [`tune.a4`](/cli/tune-a4).

  - `planner_accel` at `60000` works best with `cap_track`. Values in between would re-plan every target so the joint barely moves, and are refused.
  - `cap_track` is sent to the realtime core, not written to the motor.
  - `tf_rated_current_a` needs firmware with command 0x73 (VersionDate 2026042402 or later) and direct tracking (`planner_accel` `0`). The X8-P20 shoulders have it, and the X6-P20 elbow doesn't. Elsewhere the joint stays on plain 0xA4 and the core logs why.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stiction, dither and Stribeck terms">
  These feed-forward terms target slow-motion stick-slip on geared joints. All are off by default.

  - **Stiction push.** The peak push is `stiction_gain × fc + stiction_load_gain × abs(gravity)`. It fades on measured velocity and is gone above about 0.05 rad/s, so it never drives the slip. Keep `stiction_gain` below the breakaway-to-`fc` ratio that [`tune.breakaway`](/cli/tune-breakaway) measures.
  - **Dither.** Start `dither_nm` at 1 to 2 Nm on a shoulder. The dither is audible.
  - **Stribeck cancellation.** The term is `stribeck_gain × (stribeck_dfs + stribeck_load_gain × abs(gravity)) × exp(−(v/stribeck_vs)²) × tanh(v/0.02)`, so it is zero at rest. Raise `stribeck_gain` from 0.5 toward 0.9, and stop when the arm's 3 Hz mode grows.
  - **Stribeck pole.** `20` is smooth but lags a fast surge. `40` to `80` follows it more closely but adds encoder noise to the torque.
</Accordion>
