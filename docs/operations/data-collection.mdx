---
title: "Data Collection"
description: "Record teleoperation episodes with camera video to a LeRobot dataset for training."
---

Data collection records teleoperation episodes and camera video into a [LeRobot](/api/lerobot) dataset you can train on. Start it from the web control panel or with `axol collect-data`.

- **Axol** records joint positions by default. Turn on **Observe Cartesian** to record gripper poses instead.
- **Mantis** records gripper poses from Quest, Lighthouse or Ultimate tracking.

Run it on the computer that is wired to the hardware and has the ZED cameras attached, usually a ZED Box or Jetson. Images and actions then share one clock.

## Before you start

- Install Axol with the [one-command install](/installation) and connect the device you selected.
  - For **Axol**, connect the robot.
  - For **Mantis**, finish its [hardware](/mantis/hardware) and [pose source](/mantis/tracking) setup. **Mantis settings → Tracking** must show setup complete, with an approved tracker-to-gripper transform for both sides.
- Connect at least one ZED camera over GMSL. Assign it to a slot with **Record** turned on.
  - Axol uses the overhead, left arm and right arm slots.
  - Mantis uses separate Head, Left Mantis and Right Mantis slots.
  - Both can record the stereo head camera and both wrist cameras together.
- For Axol, and for Mantis with the Quest source, have a Meta Quest with the [TLS certificate authorized](/operations/teleop#authorize-the-tls-certificate). Mantis with Lighthouse or Ultimate needs no headset.

<Tip>
  To list the connected cameras and their serial numbers, run:

  ```bash
  python -c "from almond_axol.zed import list_zed_devices; print(list_zed_devices())"
  ```

  If a camera plugged in after boot doesn't show up, press **Restart camera service** under **Axol settings → Cameras** or **Mantis settings → Cameras**. On the CLI, run `sudo systemctl restart zed_x_daemon`.
</Tip>

## Run it

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Axol">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect the robot and assign cameras">
        Connect the **Axol Host** and, with **Axol** selected, the robot. Open **Axol settings → Cameras** and assign each camera to the overhead, left arm or right arm slot. Turn on **Record** for at least one. Axol detects stereo ZED X cameras automatically. See [Cameras](/guides/control-panel#cameras).
      </Step>

      <Step title="Select Collect data and fill in the fields">
        Pick **Collect data** and fill in:

        - **Repo ID**: type a new name such as `myorg/pick-place`, or pick an existing dataset from the list to add to it. The list shows each dataset's episode count.
        - **Task**: a description of the task.

        Recording rate, teleoperation rate, stiffness and upload settings are in the [Settings](/guides/control-panel#settings) tabs.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Start and record from the headset">
        Press **Start**, connect the headset and drive the arms. Record with the controllers, as described in [Recording an episode](#recording-an-episode). The panel shows the camera feeds and episode state, and has buttons for the same actions. See [Driving from the panel](#driving-from-the-panel).
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **From the CLI**, on the robot's computer:

    ```bash
    axol collect-data \
        --repo_id myorg/pick-place \
        --task "Pick the red cube and place it in the bin" \
        --robot_config.cameras "{overhead: {serial: 41234567}, left_arm: {serial: 41234568}, right_arm: {serial: 41234569}}"
    ```

    Replace the serial numbers with your cameras'. Assign at least one slot. Slots you leave out aren't recorded, so `--robot_config.cameras "{overhead: {serial: 41234567}}"` records the overhead camera only.

    The `cameras` value is one inline YAML dictionary. See [Command configuration](/cli/configuration#field-name-conventions).

    Axol detects stereo ZED X cameras from their serial number:

    - A stereo **overhead** camera records both eyes, as `overhead_left` and `overhead_right`.
    - A stereo **wrist** camera records its left eye under the slot name, like a mono camera.

    To override the detection, set `stereo: true` and choose the eyes:

    - `eyes: both` records `X_left` and `X_right`.
    - `eyes: left` or `eyes: right` records one eye under the slot name `X`. A forced stereo wrist camera needs `eyes: left` to stay single-eye.

    For example, to force a stereo overhead camera:

    ```bash
        --robot_config.cameras "{overhead: {serial: 41234567, stereo: true}, left_arm: {serial: 41234568}, right_arm: {serial: 41234569}}"
    ```

    Connect the headset and record from the controllers. See [Recording an episode](#recording-an-episode). Press `Ctrl+C` to finish. See [`collect-data`](/cli/collect-data) for every field, including `--fps`, `--teleop_hz`, stiffness and `--push_to_hub`.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Mantis">
    Before you record training data, pass the [Mantis tracking acceptance](/mantis/tracking#acceptance) with the same pose source. Collection refuses to start with a missing or unapproved tracker-to-gripper transform. The built-in VIVE factory transforms count as approved.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Mantis and check its settings">
        Press **Select** on the **Mantis** tile and let it connect. Click the tile's title to open **Mantis settings**, then check:

        - **Tracking**: the pose source's setup is complete. See [Mantis Tracking](/mantis/tracking).
        - **CAN**: the two channels are different.
        - **Triggers**: the [trigger endpoints](/mantis/hardware#testing-the-triggers-and-grippers) are checked.
        - **Cameras**: at least one **Head**, **Left Mantis** or **Right Mantis** camera has **Record** on. For stereo head video, turn on both **L** and **R** under **Record**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Select Collect data and fill in the fields">
        Pick **Collect data** and fill in **Repo ID** and **Task** as for Axol. **Start** stays unavailable until the pose source, both transforms, the Mantis link and the cameras are ready. The **Before you can start** list names what is missing.

        `mantis_allow_uncalibrated` is only for a calibration capture that won't be used for training.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Start and bring up the pose source">
        Press **Start**.

        - **Quest**: open [axol.almond.bot](https://axol.almond.bot) and enter VR. Left and right come from the controllers.
        - **Lighthouse or Ultimate**: power on both trackers inside the tracked area. Wait until both trackers report a pose and both triggers report in. No headset is needed.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Record with the source's controls">
        <Tabs>
          <Tab title="Quest">
            Hold the rigs at the start pose and release both controller grips. Press both grips to align and engage. Then record with **A** and **X** as on Axol (see [Recording an episode](#recording-an-episode)), or with the panel's episode buttons.
          </Tab>
          <Tab title="Lighthouse / Ultimate">
            Hold the rigs where the take should begin. **Squeeze both rig triggers together, then release both.** This aligns the rigs to the robot, engages tracking and starts the take. The grippers are powered only during a take.

            While recording, fully squeeze and release either trigger quickly:

            - **Three times** to end and save the take.
            - **Four times** to end and discard it, like the panel's **Discard episode**.

            After three squeezes, Axol waits a moment in case a fourth follows. The saved take ends where the first squeeze began, so the gesture doesn't appear in the data. Squeezing three times while idle does nothing. The panel's **Start recording** needs an engaged rig.
          </Tab>
        </Tabs>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Re-record after any tracking break">
        If a tracker loses its pose, a trigger stops reporting or tracking disengages, the rig freezes and the take fails its quality check. The panel shows why the take wasn't saved. See [Driving from the panel](#driving-from-the-panel).

        After every take, tracking disengages and the grippers turn off. To start the next take, restore both sides if needed, then repeat the start gesture wherever the rigs are. Nothing resumes automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **From the CLI**, `collect-data` starts the tracker bridge and trigger readers itself. Without `--mantis_source`, it uses the source saved in the settings.

    ```bash
    axol collect-data --mantis true --mantis_source lighthouse \
      --repo_id myorg/mantis-pick --task "Pick the part" \
      --robot_config.cameras "{overhead: {serial: 41234567}, left_arm: {serial: 41234568}, right_arm: {serial: 41234569}}"
    ```

    The `overhead` slot is the head camera. It records both stereo eyes as `overhead_left` and `overhead_right`. Leave out any slots you don't use.

    Set the source to `quest` or `ultimate` as needed. Don't start a second `tracker.bridge`. See [`collect-data`](/cli/collect-data) and the [tracker reference](/cli/tracker).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Quest controller layout

With Axol, and with Mantis on the Quest source, you record from the Quest controllers. The arm controls are the same as in [teleoperation](/operations/teleop). Data collection adds the **A** button for recording.

The control panel can take the same actions. See [Driving from the panel](#driving-from-the-panel).

<img src="/assets/quest.png" alt="Quest controller diagram" />

| # | Button | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Left grip | Press both grips together to **engage**. After that, each grip freezes or resumes the arm on its side. With **Hold grips to engage** on, hold a grip to move that arm. |
| 2 | Right grip | Same as the left grip. |
| 3 | Left trigger | Close the left gripper. While disengaged, point at a camera screen and hold to **move** it. Use both triggers on one screen to **resize** it. |
| 4 | Right trigger | Close the right gripper. While disengaged, moves and resizes camera screens like the left trigger. |
| 5 | X | **Reset** the arms to the rest pose, including out of a contact hold. Cancels the countdown. During a take, asks **Discard episode?** |
| 6 | A | **Record**: start a take after a 3-second countdown. During a take, asks **Save episode?** |
| 7 | Y | **Exit** the VR session. |
| | Right **B** | **Reset the camera screens** to where you are looking. See [Camera views](/guides/vr-interface#camera-views). |
| | Thumbsticks | **Drive [Jelly](/guides/vr-interface#jelly)** between takes, on Axol on Jelly. Jelly motion is never recorded. |

The camera feeds are shown in the headset, so you can line up takes from the robot's view.

## Recording an episode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drive to the start pose">
    Put on the headset, engage tracking and drive the arms to where the take should begin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a take">
    Press **A**. Recording starts after a 3-second countdown. Press **A** again or **X** to cancel the countdown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="End the take">
    The first press asks for confirmation in the headset, so a stray press never loses a take.

    - Press **A** for **Save episode?** Press **A** again to save, or **X** to keep recording.
    - Press **X** for **Discard episode?** Press **X** again to discard and re-record, or **A** to keep recording.

    After you confirm a save, the headset shows **Saving** and only **Y** works until the episode is written.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let the arms return to rest">
    After each take, the arms return to the rest pose on their own. The move is guarded: if an arm meets resistance, such as a gripper caught on a handle or a person, the move stops.

    1. The arms go limp in a **gravity-compensation hold**. The episode keeps saving.
    2. Guide the arms clear by hand.
    3. Press **X** to plan a new return from where they are.

    You can't record while the arms are limp. Pressing **A** reminds you to reset first.

    The grips don't engage tracking until the arms are home and the episode is saved or discarded. Then release both grips and squeeze both again. A grip held through the save doesn't count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The HUD shows **Episode N** at the top right: the number of the episode you are about to record, counting from 1.

| Headset state | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Data collection` | Driving the arms, ready to record |
| `Recording` | Recording frames to the dataset |
| `Saving` | Writing the episode to disk. Controls are blocked. |

If `--root` already holds the dataset, collection adds to it. If you stop a new session before saving any episode, Axol deletes the empty dataset.

### Contact stops

Every return to rest is guarded, including the **X** reset and the first move at startup. Set the sensitivity with **Return-to-rest contact stop** in **Axol settings → Robot** (`--teleop_config.vr_teleop_config.reset_torque_threshold`). Raise it if normal returns stop for no reason. Set `0` to turn it off.

You can also stop the arms when they push hard during a take. This check is off by default, because recording often pushes on the scene on purpose. Set **Teleop contact stop** in **Axol settings → Robot** (`--teleop_config.vr_teleop_config.teleop_torque_threshold`). The panel suggests `16` Nm.

When it trips, tracking disengages, the take is discarded and the arms go limp. Guide them clear, then press **X** and record again. [Teleoperation](/operations/teleop) uses the same settings.

These stops apply to Axol's arms. Mantis has no arms to return home. Each Mantis take starts with the source's [start gesture](/mantis/tracking#start-gesture-and-recovery), and the grippers are off between takes.

## Driving from the panel

When you start from the [control panel](/guides/control-panel), it also works as an operator display. It shows the camera feeds the headset receives, with a full-screen option. The **Episode control** box shows the phase, the episode number and the saved count, with these buttons:

- **Start recording** starts the same 3-second countdown as the headset. Click it again to cancel.
- **Save episode** and **Discard episode** ask you to click again to confirm.
- **Return to rest** appears during a contact hold. It does the same as **X**.

The controllers keep working. Whichever input arrives first ends the take. Anything you start on the controllers, such as a confirmation or a countdown, also shows in the panel. See [Episode controls](/guides/control-panel#episode-controls).

When a take ends without being saved, the **Episode control** box shows the reason in red, and the log shows it too. The message stays until the next take starts recording. A take isn't saved when:

- the Mantis quality check rejects it,
- a camera fails during capture,
- it ends before its first frame is recorded, or
- the recorder refuses it at save.

## Saved data

- **Each episode is on disk as soon as it is saved.** If the recorder stops unexpectedly, you lose at most the take being written.
- **Resuming checks the dataset.** If the saved episodes have a gap, Axol keeps the complete episodes before the gap, removes the rest and continues from there. If no complete episode is left, it asks you to delete the dataset.
- **Takes with dropped video frames are discarded.** If the camera encoder drops frames under heavy load, the video doesn't match the recorded rows. Axol discards that take and logs why. The session keeps running, so record the take again.

See the [VR Interface](/guides/vr-interface) guide for every controller binding and headset state.

## Mix Axol and Mantis episodes

Axol and Mantis episodes can go in one dataset. For each Axol run:

- Turn on **Observe Cartesian**, so the recorded state and actions match Mantis.
- Keep the gripper setting, camera slots, image resolution and recording rate the same as the Mantis runs.

A dataset started on Mantis may have an `observation.pose_lag` column, which Axol fills in automatically. A dataset started on Axol without that column keeps its layout, and Mantis leaves the column out.
