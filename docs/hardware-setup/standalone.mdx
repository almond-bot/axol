Axol ships fully assembled. In the standalone configuration you provide the table or fixture: drill or tap its top for Axol's four **M8** mounting holes using the [mounting holes diagram](https://www.almond.bot/resources/axol_mounting_holes.pdf), and make sure the surface is rigid enough to take the arms' reaction loads without flexing.

<Tip>
  If you're mounting to a T-slot table, the [Owl Mount](/hardware-setup/owl-mount) drops onto 2020, 4040, and 4545 extrusion patterns without drilling.
</Tip>

### Tools required

You'll need a hex (Allen) key for the socket head cap screws that hold Axol to your fixture:

| Screw | Hex key |
|---|---|
| M8 | 6&nbsp;mm |
