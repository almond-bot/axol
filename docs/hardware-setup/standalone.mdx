Axol ships fully assembled. In the Standalone setup you provide the table or fixture. Before you start:

- Drill or tap the top of your table for Axol's four **M8** bolts. The hole positions are in the [mounting holes diagram](https://downloads.almond.bot/resources/axol_mounting_holes.pdf).
- Make sure the table is stiff enough that it doesn't flex when the arms move.

<Tip>
  If you have a T-slot table, the [Owl Mount](/hardware-setup/owl-mount) fits 2020, 4040 and 4545 extrusion without drilling.
</Tip>

### Tools required

You need a hex (Allen) key for the bolts that hold Axol to your table:

| Screw | Hex key |
|---|---|
| M8 | 6&nbsp;mm |
