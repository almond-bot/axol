---
title: "Lidar"
description: "Connect a Livox Mid-360 lidar to the Compute Kit so Axol on Jelly can map and navigate."
---

A lidar lets Axol on Jelly build a map of its space and drive itself to saved waypoints. Axol supports the **Livox Mid-360** (also sold as the Mid-360S). It connects to the Compute Kit over Ethernet and needs its own power.

After setup, follow [Mapping and Navigation](/guides/navigation) to make a map and drive to waypoints.

## What you need

- A Livox Mid-360 with its M12 cable. The cable ends in an RJ45 plug, power leads and sync leads.
- A free Ethernet port on the Compute Kit.
- A 12 V DC supply rated for at least 15 W.
- A metal plate to mount the lidar on, and four M3 screws.

## Power

The lidar takes 9 to 27 V. It draws about 6.5 W, and up to 14 W for a few minutes when it starts cold and warms itself up.

<Warning>
  Don't power the lidar from Axol's 24 V line or straight from Jelly's battery. Both can rise above the lidar's 27 V limit: the arms push current back into the line when they slow down, and Jelly's battery reaches 29 V while it charges.
</Warning>

| Setup | Power the lidar from |
|---|---|
| Axol on Jelly | A 12 V DC-DC converter on Jelly's battery, rated for at least 15 W and for 30 V input |
| Owl Mount, Ox Cart or Standalone | A 12 V wall adapter, rated for at least 15 W |

Connect the red power lead to +12 V and the black lead to ground. Leave the sync leads unconnected.

## Mount

- Mount the lidar on a metal plate. It has no fan and cools through its base.
- Leave at least 10 mm of space around it.
- Mount it level and high enough to see over Jelly and the arms. It sees from 7° below level to 52° above, all the way around.
- Write down where it sits: how far forward of the centre of Jelly and how far to the left.

The lidar can't see the floor close to Jelly. Mounted 1 m above the floor, its view reaches the floor about 8 m away. Low objects near the base are hard for it to see.

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plug in the Ethernet cable">
    Plug the lidar's RJ45 plug into a free Ethernet port on the Compute Kit. If that port is the robot's only network connection, connect the robot to Wi-Fi first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Power it on">
    Switch on the lidar's power. It takes about 10 seconds to start, longer when it is cold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the setup">
    On the Compute Kit, run:

    ```bash
    axol lidar.setup --mount 0.2 0 0 0 0 0 --measure-mount
    ```

    Replace `0.2 0` with how far forward and to the left of Jelly's centre the lidar sits, in metres. `--measure-mount` measures the lidar's height and tilt, so keep the robot still on a level floor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    ```bash
    axol lidar.info
    axol lidar.view
    ```

    `lidar.info` should report about 200k points/s, 10 frames/s and IMU 200 Hz. `lidar.view` shows the live point cloud at `http://<robot>:8010`. The floor should sit on the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check which way it faces">
    In `lidar.view`, stand a step in front of the robot. You should appear along the red axis. If you appear somewhere else, measure the angle from where you appear to the red axis, counter-clockwise seen from above. Run the setup again with that angle as the last `--mount` value:

    ```bash
    axol lidar.setup --mount 0.2 0 0 0 0 90 --measure-mount
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

`lidar.setup` asks for your password, because it changes the network settings. It gives the lidar's port its own address, so the lidar can't clash with your network. See [`lidar.setup`](/cli/lidar-setup) for details.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| `Lidar not found` | Check the lidar's power and its Ethernet cable. Wait for it to finish starting, then run `axol lidar.setup` again. |
| `carries this computer's internet connection` | The lidar is in the port the robot uses for its network. Move the lidar to another port, or connect the robot to Wi-Fi. |
| Fewer than 200k points/s | Check the cable. Run `axol lidar.info` and look at **Faults**. |
| The floor in `lidar.view` is tilted or off the grid | Run `axol lidar.setup --measure-mount` again with the robot still on a level floor. |
