Jelly Mobile is a **powered mobile base** for Axol. It has omni-wheels that drive in any direction, a 610 mm telescoping lift and a 2.4 kWh battery. Once Axol is on Jelly, you drive the base and the lift with the headset thumbsticks during teleoperation (see [Jelly in the VR interface guide](/guides/vr-interface#jelly)).

For specs, see the [Jelly Mobile product page](https://www.almond.bot/jelly-mobile). For base and lift dimensions, see the [outline drawing](https://downloads.almond.bot/resources/jelly_outline.pdf) (PDF).

### Tools required

You need a 6&nbsp;mm hex (Allen) key for Axol. The other tools depend on which Compute Kit you have:

| Screw | Driver |
|---|---|
| M8 (Axol) | 6&nbsp;mm hex |
| M6 (Jetson AGX Orin) | T30 Torx |
| M3 (ZED Box Orin) | T10 Torx |
| ZED Box Orin mounting brackets | Phillips |

### Power panel

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/jelly_power_panel.png" alt="Jelly's power panel: on-switch, E-Stop connector, power switch, and charge connector" width="500" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The power panel is on the back of Jelly. It has four parts:

- **Green on-switch**: starts Jelly. It works only when the red power switch is on and the E-Stop is released.
- **E-Stop connector**, below the on-switch: plug the E-Stop in here. Keep the E-Stop where the operator can reach it.
- **Red power switch**: the main power switch. Turn it off to cut all power to the base.
- **Anderson connector**: the charge port. See [Charging Jelly](#charging-jelly).

<Warning>
  The E-Stop cuts power to Axol at once, and the arms drop. It does not cut power to the Jetson.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Cutting power to a running Jetson can corrupt its operating system. Shut the Jetson down from its operating system first, then turn the red power switch off.
</Note>

### Assembling Jelly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unbox">
    Set Jelly on a level floor with room to work around it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the cable chain">
    The cable chain carries Axol's cables up the lift column. It may ship with its top end loose. If it does, fasten the chain's top bracket to the top plate so the chain moves with the lift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mount the compute">
    Install the Compute Kit in the compartment in Jelly's base. Follow [Mounting the compute](#mounting-the-compute) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Power on">
    1. Plug the E-Stop into its connector on the [power panel](#power-panel) and make sure it is released.
    2. Turn the red power switch on.
    3. Press the green on-switch. The computer in the base starts up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise the lift to install height">
    Run [`axol lift.goto`](/cli/lift-goto). With no target given, it raises the lift to **75%**, the install height. At this height it is easy to slide Axol onto the mounting plate. The lift ships homed, so you don't need to calibrate it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mount Axol">
    Follow [Mounting Axol](#mounting-axol) below. You need four **M8×12 bolts** with locking washers, which thread in from under the plate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Axol">
    Plug the **XT90** power lead and the **USB-C** control cable into the back of Axol. Then fit the cameras with the [Axol Cameras](/hardware-setup/cameras) guide. You connected the camera cables to the computer when you mounted it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drive">
    During teleoperation, drive the base and the lift with the headset thumbsticks. See [Jelly in the VR interface guide](/guides/vr-interface#jelly).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Mounting the compute

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/jelly_compute.jpg" alt="The compute mounted inside Jelly's base compartment, with the power, USB, and antenna cables connected" width="500" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Compute Kit ships separately and goes inside the compartment in Jelly's base. Every cable it needs is already in the compartment, so you only plug them in:

- A **19V DC power lead** (up to **5A**) from Jelly's battery.
- The **USB-to-CAN adapters** for the wheels and the lift.
- Two **antenna cables** (the braided pair on the right in the photo).
- Axol's **USB-C** control cable.
- The Camera Kit's **GMSL cables**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mount the compute">
    Fasten the computer into the compartment with the brackets that come with it. Face the heatsink fins outward so air can flow. The bolts depend on which Compute Kit you have:

    - **ZED Box Orin**: first attach the brackets to the ZED Box with their Phillips screws. Then fasten it in with 4× **M3×12** Torx bolts (T10).
    - **Jetson AGX Orin**: 4× **M6×12** Torx bolts (T30).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect power, CAN, and antennas">
    - Plug the **19V, 5A** DC power lead into the computer's power input.
    - Plug the wheel and lift **USB-to-CAN** adapters into its USB ports.
    - Connect the two **antenna cables** to its antenna connectors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Axol and the cameras">
    Plug Axol's USB-C control cable into a USB port on the computer. Plug the camera cables into its **GMSL2** inputs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Charging Jelly

Jelly's **2.4 kWh** battery lasts **4 to 10 hours**, depending on how much you drive and use the lift. Charge it through the **Anderson connector** on the power panel with the charger in the box.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power off">
    Stop driving, shut the Jetson down from its operating system, then turn the red power switch off. The lift holds its height without power, so you can leave it where it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the charger">
    Plug the charger into a wall outlet, then into the Anderson connector on the power panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for full">
    Leave the charger connected until it shows the battery is full. Unplug the charger before you turn Jelly back on.
  </Step>
</Steps>
