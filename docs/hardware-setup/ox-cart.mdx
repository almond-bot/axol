The Ox Cart is a **height-adjustable mobile cart** with four leveling casters and a manual crank lift. It ships with the crank handle, and the casters are already fitted. For specs, see the [Ox Cart product page](https://www.almond.bot/ox-cart).

### Tools required

You need a hex (Allen) key for the bolts that hold Axol to the cart:

| Screw | Hex key |
|---|---|
| M8 | 6&nbsp;mm |

### Leveling the casters

Each caster has a ratchet with a small lever that raises or lowers the wheel. To level the cart, flip the ratchet switch up or down on each caster and work the lever until the base sits level.

### Raising and lowering the robot

To change Axol's height, insert the crank into the hole at the top of the cart and turn it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/height_adjust.png" alt="Inserting the crank into the Ox Cart to adjust its height" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Push the crank **all the way in** before you turn it. A crank that is only partly in can slip or damage the lift.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Before you mount Axol, crank the cart down to a height that is easy to lift to. Raise it to working height after the bolts are tight.
</Tip>
