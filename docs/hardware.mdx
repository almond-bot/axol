You choose your Axol setup when you order. Every setup has these parts:

- **Axol**: the dual-arm robot. Every configuration starts here.
- **Mount**: one of the following.
  - **Standalone**: bolt Axol to your own table or fixture.
  - **Owl Mount**: a rigid column that bolts to a table or fixture.
  - **Ox Cart**: height-adjustable mobile cart with a manual lift and leveling casters.
  - **Jelly Mobile**: powered mobile base with a battery and telescoping lift.
- **Camera Kit** (optional): two ZED X One S Fisheye wrist cameras and one ZED X Mini stereo head camera, with mounts and GMSL2 cables.
- **Compute Kit**: the NVIDIA Jetson computer that runs Axol. It comes in three sizes:
  - **ZED Box Orin**: Jetson Orin NX 16GB in the industrial ZED Box.
  - **Jetson AGX Orin**: 64GB, for running the full Camera Kit.
  - **Jetson Thor**: 128GB Blackwell, for VLA policies and the full Camera Kit on one box.

## Axol Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/axol.png" alt="Almond Axol dual-arm robot" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

Axol ships fully assembled and ready to mount. It attaches with four **M8×12 bolts** that thread in from below. The hole positions are in the [mounting holes diagram](#reference-diagrams).

The back of the robot has these connectors:

- **2× GMSL2 ports** for the wrist cameras.
- **1× XT90 connector** for power (**24V DC, 60A peak**).
- **1× locking USB-C connector** for control.

Axol works with two grippers:

- **Parallel**: the standard two-finger gripper.
- **Parcel**: a gripper with hinged plates, used by [box mode](/guides/vr-interface#box-mode).

Tell the software which gripper is fitted. Use the **Gripper** setting under **Axol settings → Robot** in the control panel, or pass `--axol.gripper parallel` or `--axol.gripper parcel` on the command line (see [`AxolConfig.gripper`](/api/robot)). When the arms first enable after power-up, Axol measures each gripper's travel against its end stops.

## Owl Mount Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/owl_mount.png" alt="Almond Axol on the Owl Mount" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Owl Mount is a rigid **400mm 8080 aluminum T-slot column** that holds Axol on a table, workbench or fixture. Its base brackets have holes for **2020, 4040 and 4545** extrusion patterns, so it fits most T-slot tables without drilling. The slots on the column stay free for cables and accessories.

The Owl Mount comes with an Axol mounting plate and all the bolts you need to fix it to your table.

## Ox Cart Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/ox_cart.png" alt="Almond Axol on the height-adjustable Ox Cart" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Ox Cart is a sturdy mobile cart with **four leveling casters** and a **manual crank lift**. Roll Axol between workstations, lock the casters, and crank it to a working height anywhere from sitting to standing.

The **60 × 60 cm** footprint fits through standard doors. The frame is **powder-coated steel** with T-slots, so you can add your own fixtures. The cart ships with its crank handle and casters.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Footprint** | 60 × 60 cm |
| **Height range** | Sit-to-stand, manual crank |
| **Wheels** | 4× leveling casters |
| **Weight** | 54.5 kg |
| **Mounting** | T-slot extrusion |

## Jelly Mobile Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/axol_mount_jelly.png" alt="Almond Axol on the Jelly Mobile base" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

Jelly Mobile is a **powered mobile base** for Axol. It has omni-wheels that drive in any direction, a **telescoping lift**, and a **2.4 kWh battery**, so Axol can work all day without a power cable. Its universal mounting plate also fits other robots, and its **600 × 600 mm** footprint fits through standard doors.

During teleoperation you drive the base and the lift with the headset thumbsticks. Axol detects Jelly on its own when Jelly is connected. See [Jelly in the VR interface guide](/guides/vr-interface#jelly).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Drive** | Powered holonomic drive, 0.7 m/s |
| **Lift travel** | 610 mm (2 ft) at 40 mm/s |
| **Height range** | 760–1,370 mm (2.5–4.5 ft) |
| **Footprint** | 600 × 600 mm |
| **Battery** | 2.4 kWh, 4–10 hours runtime |
| **In the box** | 1× Jelly Mobile base with battery, 1× charger |

## Camera Kit Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/axol_perception_kit.png" alt="Almond Axol with the Camera Kit cameras and head mount" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Camera Kit adds wrist and head cameras. You need cameras to collect data and to run policies. The kit contains:

- **2× ZED X One S Fisheye** wrist cameras (global shutter, 204° field of view) with wrist mounts.
- **1× ZED X Mini** stereo head camera (global shutter) with a head mount.
- **FAKRA GMSL2 cables**: three 1.5 m cables and two 0.3 m right-angle cables.

The cameras plug into the GMSL2 inputs on the Compute Kit.

## Compute Kit Overview

The Compute Kit is the NVIDIA Jetson computer that runs Axol, records the cameras and runs policies. It comes in three sizes:

| | ZED Box Orin | Jetson AGX Orin | Jetson Thor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Module** | Jetson Orin NX 16GB | Jetson AGX Orin 64GB | Jetson T5000 (Blackwell) 128GB |
| **AI performance** | 100 TOPS | 275 TOPS | 2070 FP4 TFLOPS |
| **Storage** | 256 GB SSD | 500 GB SSD | See product page |
| **Camera inputs** | 4× GMSL2 | 4× GMSL2 | 8× GMSL2 |
| **Axol control** | USB-to-CAN | USB-to-CAN | Direct CAN |
| **Best for** | The full Camera Kit at a reduced frame rate | The full Camera Kit at full frame rate | The full Camera Kit at full frame rate, plus running policies |

### Reference

- [Compute product page](https://www.almond.bot/compute)
