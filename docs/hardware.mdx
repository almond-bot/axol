Axol is configured at order time from these choices:

- **Axol**: the dual-arm robot. Every configuration starts here.
- **Mounting**: one of the following.
  - **Standalone**: bolt Axol to your own table or fixture.
  - **Owl Mount**: rigid table/fixture column.
  - **Ox Cart**: height-adjustable mobile cart with a manual lift and leveling casters.
  - **Jelly Mobile**: powered mobile base with a battery and telescoping lift.
- **Camera Kit**: 2× ZED X One S Fisheye wrist cameras and 1× ZED X Mini stereo head camera, with mounts and GMSL2 cabling.
- **Compute Kit**: the onboard Jetson that runs Axol, one of the following.
  - **ZED Box Orin**: Jetson Orin NX 16GB in the industrial ZED Box.
  - **Jetson AGX Orin**: 64GB, for running the full Camera Kit.
  - **Jetson Thor**: 128GB Blackwell, for VLA policies and the full Camera Kit on one box.

## Axol Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/axol.png" alt="Almond Axol dual-arm robot" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

Axol ships fully assembled and ready to mount. It attaches with four **M8×12 bolts** that thread in from the bottom. See the [mounting holes diagram](#reference-diagrams) below.

The back of the robot exposes the connectors used for power and camera control:

- **2× GMSL2 ports** for the wrist cameras.
- **1× XT90 connector** for power (**24V DC, 60A peak**).
- **1× Locking USB-C connector** for control.

## Owl Mount Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/owl_mount.png" alt="Almond Axol on the Owl Mount" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Owl Mount is a rigid **400mm 8080 aluminum T-slot extrusion column** that bolts Axol to a table, workbench, or fixture. Its base brackets include clearance holes for **2020, 4040, and 4545** extrusion patterns, so it drops onto most T-slot tables without drilling, and the column's T-slots stay free for cable management and accessories. An Axol mounting plate and all table/fixture hardware are included.

## Ox Cart Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/ox_cart.png" alt="Almond Axol on the height-adjustable Ox Cart" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Ox Cart is a rugged, height-adjustable mobile cart with **four leveling casters** and a **manual crank lift**, so you can roll the robot between workstations, lock it in place, and set it to a comfortable working height, from sitting to standing. Its **60x60cm footprint** fits through standard doors, the **powder-coated steel** frame stays stable under demanding workloads, and T-slot mounting keeps it easy to customize. The cart ships with its crank handle and casters.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Footprint** | 60 × 60 cm |
| **Height range** | Sit-to-stand, manual crank |
| **Wheels** | 4× leveling casters |
| **Weight** | 54.5 kg |
| **Mounting** | T-slot extrusion |

## Jelly Mobile Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/axol_mount_jelly.png" alt="Almond Axol on the Jelly Mobile base" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

Jelly Mobile is a **powered mobile base** with a **holonomic (x-drive omni-wheel) drive**, an integrated **telescoping lift**, and a **2.4 kWh battery** for all-day, untethered operation. It is built for Axol but carries any robot on its universal mounting plate. Its **600 × 600 mm footprint** fits through standard doors.

In teleop the headset thumbsticks drive Jelly's base and lift, and the SDK detects Jelly automatically from the CAN devices attached. See [Jelly in the VR interface guide](/guides/vr-interface#jelly) and [`can.setup`](/cli/can-setup).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Drive** | Powered holonomic drive, 0.7 m/s |
| **Lift travel** | 610 mm (2 ft) at 40 mm/s |
| **Height range** | 760–1,370 mm (2.5–4.5 ft) |
| **Footprint** | 600 × 600 mm |
| **Battery** | 2.4 kWh, 4–10 hours runtime |
| **In the box** | 1× Jelly Mobile base with battery, 1× charger |

## Camera Kit Overview

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/axol_perception_kit.png" alt="Almond Axol with the Camera Kit cameras and head mount" width="400" style={{ margin: "0 auto" }} />
</Frame>

The Camera Kit adds the wrist and head cameras for data collection and policy inference:

- **2× ZED X One S Fisheye** wrist cameras (global shutter, 204° FOV) with wrist mounts.
- **1× ZED X Mini** stereo head camera (global shutter) with a head mount.
- **FAKRA GMSL2 cabling**: three 1.5 m cables plus two 0.3 m right-angle cables.

The cameras connect to the Compute Kit's GMSL2 inputs.

## Compute Kit Overview

The Compute Kit is the onboard NVIDIA Jetson that runs Axol, captures the cameras, and runs policies. Three tiers are offered:

| | ZED Box Orin | Jetson AGX Orin | Jetson Thor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Module** | Jetson Orin NX 16GB | Jetson AGX Orin 64GB | Jetson T5000 (Blackwell) 128GB |
| **AI performance** | 100 TOPS | 275 TOPS | 2070 FP4 TFLOPS |
| **Storage** | 256 GB SSD | 500 GB SSD | See product page |
| **Camera inputs** | 4× GMSL2 | 4× GMSL2 | 8× GMSL2 |
| **Axol control** | USB-to-CAN | USB-to-CAN | Direct CAN |
| **Best for** | Runs the full Camera Kit at reduced frame rate | Runs the full Camera Kit at full frame rate | Runs the full Camera Kit at full frame rate + policy inference |

### Reference

- [Compute product page](https://www.almond.bot/compute)
