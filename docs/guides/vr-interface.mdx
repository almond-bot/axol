The VR interface is a WebXR app that runs in the Meta Quest browser. It sends your controller poses to Axol during [teleoperation](/operations/teleop) and [data collection](/operations/data-collection).

## Connect the headset

You can open the app from two places:

| | URL | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hosted** | [axol.almond.bot](https://axol.almond.bot) | Most setups. |
| **Self-hosted** | `https://<host>:8001/` | Served by [`axol serve`](/cli/serve) on the Host Computer. Use it offline or when you change the app. |

Either way, the headset connects to port 8000 on the Host Computer. That port is open while [`axol teleop`](/cli/teleop), [`axol collect-data`](/cli/collect-data), or a teleop or data collection operation from the [control panel](/guides/control-panel) is running.

1. Open the app in the Quest browser.
2. Enter the robot computer's address in **Host Computer address**. Tap the field to pick one of the last five hosts you connected to.
3. Press **Connect**.
4. The first time, the connection fails because the certificate is self-signed. Tap **Authorize certificate**, accept the warning in the popup, and the app reconnects. You can also open `https://<host>:8000` in the headset browser and accept the warning there.
5. Press **Enter VR**.

<Note>
  If Wi-Fi adds lag, tick **Quest over USB** before you connect. Controller poses then travel over a USB cable, and the cameras keep using the network. See [Quest over USB](/guides/quest-over-usb).
</Note>

## Camera views

When camera video is on, the robot's cameras appear as screens over passthrough. Camera video is on with [`axol teleop --cameras`](/cli/teleop), with [`axol collect-data`](/cli/collect-data), or when cameras are assigned in the control panel's [Cameras tab](/guides/control-panel#cameras).

- The overhead camera is in the center. A stereo ZED X overhead camera shows in 3D.
- The left and right wrist cameras are in the bottom corners.
- Only the cameras being streamed appear.
- **Connecting cameras…** shows until every streamed camera has sent its first frame.

The screens stay fixed in the room, where you were looking when the session started. While arm tracking is disengaged, you can arrange them:

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Move a screen | Point a controller at it, hold the trigger, and move the controller. |
| Resize a screen | Hold both triggers on the same screen, then move your hands apart or together. |
| Reset all screens | Press **B**. The screens move to where you are looking and go back to their default size. |

## Controller bindings

<img src="/assets/quest.png" alt="Quest controller diagram" />

| # | Button | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Left grip | Press both grips together to engage tracking. Once engaged, click a grip to freeze or resume its arm. |
| 2 | Right grip | Same as the left grip. |
| 3 | Left trigger | Close the left gripper. With tracking disengaged, move or resize a camera screen. |
| 4 | Right trigger | Close the right gripper. With tracking disengaged, move or resize a camera screen. |
| 5 | X | Return the arms to the rest pose. Cancels a recording countdown. While recording, asks to discard the episode. |
| 6 | A | Data collection only. Starts recording, or while recording, asks to save the episode. |
| 7 | Y | Exit VR. The arms stop and hold their position. |
| | B (right) | Reset the camera screens. |
| | Left thumbstick | Drive [Jelly](#jelly) forward, back and sideways. |
| | Right thumbstick, left or right | Turn Jelly. |
| | Left thumbstick click, held | Lower Jelly's lift. |
| | Right thumbstick click, held | Raise Jelly's lift. |
| | Both thumbstick clicks together | Turn [box mode](#box-mode) on or off. Parcel gripper only. |

### Engage and freeze the arms

- Press both grips together to start tracking.
- Click one grip to freeze that arm. It holds its pose and its grip, so it can steady an object while the other arm works. Click the grip again to resume.
- A reset, a [contact stop](/operations/teleop) or a dropped link disengages both arms. Press both grips to engage again.

With **Hold grips to engage** turned on in the control panel (`--teleop.hold_to_engage true`), each grip is a dead-man switch. Hold a grip to move its arm, and release it to freeze the arm. Either grip engages its own arm, including from rest. After a forced disengage, release a grip you are still holding before it engages again.

### If the link drops

The arms disengage after about half a second and hold their position if the headset stops sending poses. This happens when you take the headset off, open the system menu, exit the app or lose Wi-Fi. Engaging again never jerks the arms toward the controllers (see [Re-engaging](#re-engaging)). Press **X** to return them to rest.

### Teleop or data collection

The command that starts the session sets the mode. You can't switch it in the headset.

- [`axol teleop`](/cli/teleop): the HUD shows `Teleop` and **A** does nothing.
- [`axol collect-data`](/cli/collect-data): the HUD shows `Data collection` and **A** records. See [Record episodes](#record-episodes).

<a id="re-engaging"></a>

## Re-engaging

When a grip engages an arm again, your controller and the arm are usually in different places. This happens after you freeze an arm, walk away, or someone moves the arm by hand. The **Ramp** button on the HUD chooses what happens:

| Button | What happens | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramp: OFF** (clutch, the default) | The arm stays where it is. Your controller's current pose becomes the new starting point. | The arm is holding something, or you want to continue from where it is. Move your hand to roughly match the arm first, so your reach feels the same. |
| **Ramp: ON** (ramp) | The arm moves to where your controller is, using the hand-to-arm offset from its last engage. Then it follows your hand. | After a hand-off or after someone guides the arm by hand. Hold your hands where you want the arms and press the grips. |

In ramp mode the arm moves at about 0.15 m/s and takes at least 0.75 s. You can keep moving while it catches up. After a reset or a return to rest, the next engage behaves like clutch, so the arms never jump out of rest.

Set the startup default with the control panel's **Re-engage** setting or `--teleop.reengage`. During a session, use the **Ramp** button or **Re-engage** in [session settings](#session-settings).

<a id="box-mode"></a>

## Box mode

<Note>
  Box mode needs the parcel gripper. Set **Robot → Gripper** to `parcel` in the control panel, or pass `--axol.gripper parcel`. With the standard parallel gripper, box mode and its settings are hidden in the headset and the control panel.
</Note>

Box mode carries a box with both arms. The grippers press on opposite sides of the box, like two flat hands, and hold it by friction. One controller moves both arms together as a pair. The pair follows your hand's position, and turning your hand about any axis turns the pair about its center. Line the pair up with a box, then tip or tilt it once it's held.

### Turn box mode on

Use any of these:

- The **Box** button on the HUD.
- Click both thumbsticks together.
- **Box mode** in [session settings](#session-settings).

To start every session in box mode, turn on **Box mode at startup** in the control panel (`--teleop.box_mode true`).

The pair engages as soon as box mode turns on, led by your right hand. Set **Box lead hand** to `left` to lead with the left hand. If someone turns box mode on from the control panel while nobody is wearing the headset, the pair waits for a grip.

On engage, the arms first move into a parallel pair over about 1.5 s. They keep their current midpoint and width. Then the leading hand moves the pair.

<Tip>
  Bring the grippers roughly into the grasp with normal two-arm teleop before you switch. The button turns blue and reads **Box: OFF (aligned)** when the grippers face each other across a box-sized gap. Switching then barely moves the arms.
</Tip>

### Box mode controls

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Leading controller | Move it to move the pair. Turn, tip or roll it to turn the pair about its center. |
| Leading grip | Freeze the pair. Press either grip to lead again. |
| Other grip | Hand the lead to that controller. |
| Left trigger | Close the left gripper, whichever hand leads. |
| Right trigger | Close the right gripper, whichever hand leads. |
| Right thumbstick, forward or back | Change the grip width. Forward is wider, back is narrower. |
| Thumbstick click, quick tap | Switch between the straight and flush grasp. See [Grasps](#box-grasp). |
| Thumbstick click, held | Move Jelly's lift, as outside box mode. |
| Other thumbstick directions | Drive [Jelly](#jelly), as outside box mode. |
| Both thumbstick clicks | Leave box mode. |
| X | Return to rest and leave box mode. |

The width stick works only while a grip leads, and it never moves the pair. A tap is a click released within about 0.3 s, with the other stick not pressed. Hold a click longer and the lift starts moving, so one press never does both.

To clamp a box, lead the pair to it. Then pull the right thumbstick back until the gripper faces close on the box's sides. The width is the gap between the two contact faces, up to 0.70 m. The parcel gripper's faces can close all the way together, so thin parcels clamp too.

<a id="box-grasp"></a>

### Grasps

| Grasp | How the grippers sit |
|---|---|
| `straight` (default) | Fingers point straight forward. The parcel gripper's folded plate lies flat on the box. |
| `flush` | Each gripper turns 39° inward about the front edge of its plate. The plate's chamfer and the tip of the fixed blade both touch the box. Release the triggers before you close the width. |

Tap either thumbstick while leading to switch, or set **Box grasp** in [session settings](#session-settings). The arms move to the other grasp over about 1.5 s, and you can switch with a box already clamped. The **Box** button shows the current grasp, for example **Box: ON (straight)**. Set the flush angle with **Angled yaw** and the starting grasp with `--teleop.box_grasp`.

<a id="box-tool"></a>

### The parcel gripper

The parcel gripper has a fixed blade and a hinged plate. When you release the trigger, the plate opens fully and folds back along the wrist, on the side facing the box. Box mode places each gripper by this contact face, not by its mount.

Each gripper turns whichever flat side is closer toward the box, so the wrist never makes a half turn. If a gripper engages with its fixed blade toward the box, or with its motor cap facing down, set **Left face** or **Right face** to `+x` or `-x`. Try the other value if the first is wrong, then engage again. If the pair doesn't sit flat on the box, adjust **Angled yaw** or `--teleop.box_grip_tilt`.

<a id="driving-with-the-box"></a>

### Driving with the box

The thumbsticks drive [Jelly](#jelly) with the normal controls the whole time box mode is on, whether the pair is led or frozen. Only the right thumbstick's forward and back sets the width instead. You can carry a box across the room while you lead the pair.

For a long drive, freeze the pair:

1. Clamp the box.
2. Click the leading grip to freeze the pair. The arms hold the box where it is, with the grippers closed, whatever your hands do.
3. Drive to where you need to go.
4. Squeeze both triggers, then press a grip to lead the pair again.

A pair you have tipped keeps its tilt when you freeze it, hand over the lead or switch the grasp. Only turning box mode on again squares the grippers up level.

<Warning>
  When a grip takes the lead, each gripper follows its trigger again. If the triggers are released at that moment, the grippers open and the box drops.
</Warning>

<a id="box-elbows"></a>

### Elbows

Set **Elbows out** in [session settings](#session-settings). It goes from 0° (hanging below the line from shoulder to wrist) to 90° (level), and starts at 30°. Bring the elbows in to pass a doorway, or out for a wide box.

If closing the width would bring an arm too close to the torso, the width stops closing. To turn off elbow control, pass `--teleop.box_elbow_weight 0`. The elbows then rest where they fall, and **Elbows out** does nothing.

### Clamp force

The clamp gets harder the further you push the width past the box, by about 6 N per arm for each centimetre. These settings shape the squeeze. They work on the real robot only.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Squeeze force** | 0 (off) | Caps the clamp force per arm, in newtons. Try 8 N for a light parcel, and raise it if boxes slip. |
| **Squeeze lean** | 1× | Tilts each gripper into the box as you squeeze, so the blade tip and the plate press evenly. Raise it if the tip lifts, lower it if the plate lifts. 0 turns it off. |
| **Squeeze trim** | 5° | The most extra inward turn added while pressing, so each gripper face ends up flat on the box. 0 turns it off. |
| **Squeeze cap** | 0 (off) | Caps the shoulder torque that squeezes the box. Leave it off unless you need it, because it also slows an arm moving toward the other and skews the pair. |

With **Squeeze force** off, nothing limits the clamp. Past about 39 N per arm, the blade tip can lift and the plate's chamfer takes the whole load. Set **Squeeze force** to keep the clamp below that.

While the grippers press, the **Box** button shows the clamp force and trim, for example **Box: ON (straight, 6 N, 2° in)**. The teleop log prints a `box clamp:` line every second.

### Leave box mode

Click both thumbsticks together, press the **Box** button, or turn off **Box mode** in the control panel. Press both clicks within about a third of a second of each other. A quick double click counts as one toggle.

Both arms engage in normal teleop as box mode turns off:

- With **Ramp: ON**, each arm moves back to where its controller is, using the hand-to-arm offset from before box mode.
- With **Ramp: OFF**, the arms stay where they are and follow your hands from there.

If you leave while leading in the `flush` grasp, the pair first returns to `straight` over about 1.5 s. To leave right away, release the grip or toggle again. Pressing **X** always leaves box mode and returns the arms to rest.

After a hand-off, or after someone moves the arms by hand, press a grip. Box mode always starts by moving the arms into the parallel pair at their current midpoint and width.

<a id="session-settings"></a>

## Session settings

You can change these settings during a session. Open them with the **Settings** button on the HUD, or in the **Session settings** card of the control panel. Both show the same values, so a change in one appears in the other. **Box** and **Ramp** also have their own HUD buttons.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Box mode** | One controller drives both arms as a pair. See [Box mode](#box-mode). |
| **Re-engage** | `clutch` or `ramp`. See [Re-engaging](#re-engaging). |
| **Hold to engage** | Makes each grip a dead-man switch instead of a toggle. |
| **Elbows out** (°) | Box mode. How far out the elbows are held, from 0 to 90. |
| **Squeeze lean** (×) | Box mode. How far each gripper tilts into the box as you squeeze. Real robot only. |
| **Squeeze trim** (°) | Box mode. The most extra inward turn added while pressing. Real robot only. |
| **Box lead hand** | Which controller leads the pair when box mode turns on. |
| **Box grasp** | `straight` or `flush`. |
| **Angled yaw** (°) | How far the `flush` grasp turns each gripper inward. Default 39. |
| **Left face** / **Right face** | `auto`, `+x` or `-x`. Which side of each gripper faces the box. |
| **Squeeze force** (N) | Box mode. Caps the clamp force per arm. 0 means no cap. Real robot only. |
| **Squeeze cap** (Nm) | Box mode. Caps the shoulder torque that squeezes the box. 0 is off. Real robot only. |
| **Grip force** (Nm) | How hard a closed gripper squeezes, on both hands. Real robot only. |
| **Reach scale** (×) | Hand motion multiplier. Above 1 extends your reach, below 1 gives finer control. |
| **Arm speed** (rev/s) | Joint speed limit while tracking. Lower it for slow, careful work. |

The box mode settings appear only with the parcel gripper. Sessions start with the values from the command line or the control panel's saved settings. Changes made here last for the current session only.

<a id="jelly"></a>

## Jelly

On Axol on Jelly, the thumbsticks drive the base, so you can move Axol without leaving VR. Jelly is a powered base with omni wheels and a telescoping lift.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Left thumbstick | Drive forward, back and sideways. |
| Right thumbstick, left or right | Turn. |
| Left thumbstick click, held | Lower the lift. |
| Right thumbstick click, held | Raise the lift. |

- Jelly moves only while you push a stick, and stops smoothly when you let go. This works whether or not arm tracking is engaged.
- Speed follows how far you push the stick. The first part of the travel is slow, for creeping up on a spot, and a full push is full speed. Set the curve with `--jelly.stick_expo` (default `0.6`, `0` is linear).
- In box mode, the sticks still drive Jelly. The right thumbstick's forward and back sets the grip width instead, and a held click must last about 0.3 s before the lift moves. See [Driving with the box](#driving-with-the-box).
- If the headset link drops while you drive, Jelly stops. A parked Jelly keeps holding its position.
- After several minutes of driving mostly one way, a parked Jelly brakes its wheels instead of holding them, and it can creep slowly on a slope. Power Jelly off and on to restore the full hold.
- If a parked wheel turns fast or moves too far, Jelly turns its wheels off and stops parking until you restart the session.
- Jelly's motion is never recorded in a dataset, and a policy never drives it. You can move Axol during teleop or data collection without affecting the recording.

### Set up Jelly

Axol drives Jelly when Jelly's CAN adapters are plugged in. The wheels use `can_alm_axol_b`. The lift uses `can_alm_axol_c`, or shares the wheel bus. The control panel names the adapters when they first appear ([CAN discovery](/guides/control-panel#axol-and-mantis)), or you can run [`axol can.setup`](/cli/can-setup).

To leave attached hardware out of a session, use the switches in the **Robot** tab of **Axol settings**:

| Switch | Flag |
|---|---|
| **Axol arms** | `--arms false` |
| **Jelly wheels** | `--jelly.wheels false` |
| **Jelly lift** | `--jelly.lift false` |

With **Axol arms** off, the session drives only Jelly and never connects to the arms. The same happens when Jelly is attached but the arm hub is not. Jelly's other settings are under **Advanced → Jelly**, or the `--jelly.*` flags of [`axol teleop`](/cli/teleop).

Home the lift once with [`axol lift.home`](/cli/lift-home) before it can move. [`axol lift.goto`](/cli/lift-goto) raises it to the install height. Both are also in the [diagnostics dashboard](/guides/diagnostics-dashboard#helpers).

## Record episodes

In data collection, record with **A** and **X**:

1. Press **A**. Recording starts after a 3-second countdown. Press **X** during the countdown to cancel.
2. To finish, press **A**. **Save episode?** appears. Press **A** again to save, or **X** to keep recording.
3. To throw the take away, press **X**. **Discard episode?** appears. Press **X** again to discard it and record again, or **A** to keep recording.

Nothing is saved until you confirm. While an episode is being saved, every control except **Y** is blocked. The HUD shows **Episode N** at the top right, the number of the episode you are about to record. If the robot reports an error, the headset shows an error indicator. For the messages behind these states, see the [VR server API](/api/vr).

## Run the app yourself

The app's source is in the `web/` folder of this repository. The same build produces the [control panel](/guides/control-panel). From `web/`:

```bash
npm install

# Build the client package first, then the app (output: web/app/dist, served by axol serve)
npm run build --workspace=packages/axol-vr-client
npm run build --workspace=app

# Development server with hot reload
npm run dev --workspace=app
```

For development, open the printed URL in the Quest browser and connect as usual.
