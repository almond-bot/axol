---
title: "diag.base-calibrate"
description: "Calibrate the cart's per-wheel radii with the overhead ZED's positional tracking."
---

Measures how much each of the cart's four omni wheels actually differs in effective radius and turns that into the `--cart.wheel_scale` correction, with no tape measure or floor marks. A few percent of radius spread is invisible from the wheels themselves — each tracks its commanded speed perfectly — but slides the base sideways by centimetres per metre even with the gyro heading hold engaged, because the wheels' surface speeds are mutually inconsistent and the free rollers absorb the difference.

The cart drives itself through six short strokes from wherever it stands — spin 90° counter-clockwise and back, then forward, back, left and right by `--distance` — with the heading hold off. For each stroke it pairs the wheels' net rotation (motor odometry) with the camera's measured displacement and heading change (the ZED SDK's GEN_3 visual-inertial tracking with depth computation off since the tracker doesn't use it: 60 Hz, tens of microns of stationary noise), then solves the x-drive kinematics by least squares for the four radii. The camera's offset from the drive centre, its mounting yaw and the wheelbase are fitted alongside, so nothing about the mount needs measuring; the mount's pitch doesn't matter.

Run it on the ZED box with the wheels and the overhead camera both free (stop `axol serve` / teleop first), on the flattest floor available with about 2 m clear on every side:

```bash
axol diag.base-calibrate                     # 1.5 m strokes, prompts before moving
axol diag.base-calibrate --distance 2 --repeat 2 --save   # longer, averaged, written to settings
axol diag.base-calibrate --out strokes.json  # keep the raw strokes
axol diag.base-calibrate --refit strokes.json --lever 0.42   # refit offline, wheelbase pinned
```

The report lists every stroke with its fit residual, the effective radii in millimetres, the resulting `wheel_scale`, the fitted camera mount and lever arm, and the line to paste into `--cart.wheel_scale` (or **Advanced → Cart → wheel_scale** in the control panel; `--save` writes it there directly). Residuals above a few millimetres mean a wheel slipped or the camera lost track during a stroke — an unloaded diagonal on a bad patch of floor — and the run should be repeated on flatter ground rather than trusted. `--save` refuses a suspect fit unless confirmed.

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `--distance M`, `--angle DEG` | Translation stroke length (default 1.5 m) and spin size (default 90°). Longer strokes average out tracking noise. |
| `--speed`, `--turn-speed` | Normalized commands, as a fraction of `CartConfig.max_speed` (defaults 0.25 / 0.2). Keep them gentle: slip during a stroke is exactly what the fit cannot model. |
| `--repeat N` | Run the six-stroke sequence N times; all strokes go into one fit. |
| `--serial` | Stereo ZED to track with. Defaults to the configured overhead camera, else the only stereo ZED connected. Positional tracking needs a stereo ZED X; a mono ZED-X One won't do. |
| `--lever M` | Wheel rotation lever arm `(a+b)/√2` if known; otherwise fitted from the spins. |
| `--tracker`, `--depth-mode`, `--resolution` | ZED tracking configuration: `gen3` (default) / `gen3-2d` are the SDK's visual-inertial SLAM (no depth needed; `2d` adds a ground-plane constraint that never initializes if the base rocks), `gen2` / `gen1` the depth-based odometers (pair with `--depth-mode PERFORMANCE`; both deprecated in SDK 5.x, and `gen2` dropped tracking mid-spin on the cart). Use the report's **tracking consistency** table to compare: within one stroke direction the ratio of camera motion to wheel rotation must repeat to within a few percent, and translation strokes must point along their axis. A camera looking down at a plain floor sees little parallax for motion along its own optical axis, so forward/back is the direction to watch. |
| `--out FILE`, `--refit FILE` | Dump the raw strokes as JSON / fit a dump without driving. |
| `--save`, `-y` | Persist `cart.wheel_scale` into the control panel's saved settings; skip the confirmation prompts. |

Wheel scale is a property of the wheels, not the floor — recalibrate when a wheel is replaced or after heavy wear, not per room. Also launchable from the [diagnostics dashboard](https://docs.almond.bot/guides/diagnostics-dashboard).
