---
title: "nav.waypoint"
description: "List, add and delete the waypoints saved on a map."
---

Lists a map's waypoints, or adds and deletes them. A waypoint is a place on the map and the direction to face there.

```bash
axol nav.waypoint office
axol nav.waypoint office add bench 4.2 -1.5 90
axol nav.waypoint office add dock --here
axol nav.waypoint office remove bench
```

`add` takes the position in metres and the heading in degrees, counter-clockwise from the map's x axis. `--here` saves where the robot is now. Keep the robot still while it finds itself.

To save waypoints while you drive, use **Save current position** in the [`nav.run`](/cli/nav-run) map view. Waypoints are saved in `~/.almond/maps/NAME/waypoints.json`.
