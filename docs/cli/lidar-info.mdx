---
title: "lidar.info"
description: "Show the lidar's status and measure its data rate."
---

Prints the lidar's model, serial number, firmware, address, temperature and faults, then measures its data rate for two seconds.

```bash
axol lidar.info
axol lidar.info --json
axol lidar.info --seconds 5
```

A healthy Mid-360 reports about 200k points/s, 10 frames/s and IMU 200 Hz. Run [`lidar.setup`](/cli/lidar-setup) first.
